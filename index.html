Our first project

The Great American Story

A 3D immersive app for Apple Vision Pro.

An educational and experiential app that lets viewers step inside breathtaking, immersive stories filmed across the US. Rather than watching a documentary on a screen, viewers can virtually stand in the scene itself — beside a park ranger at a geyser basin, under the cherry blossoms at the Tidal Basin in DC, on stage with a Broadway actor in New York — and hear the story told as if they were there in person.

How it's filmed

A sense of presence that traditional film can't achieve.

Every story is captured with the Blackmagic URSA Cine Immersive, one of the most advanced cameras released to date.

  • 16K, 3D, 180° immersive videoLifelike depth and detail that fills your entire field of view.
  • Spatial audioSound comes from where it happened.
  • Made for Apple Immersive VideoDesigned specifically for Apple Vision Pro's format.

Viewers don't watch the story — they're in it.

Who we are

Ambidocs

A production company dedicated to immersive, place-based storytelling. Our mission is to tell American stories in the most vivid medium ever created — so that anyone, anywhere, can experience them firsthand.

Eammon Atkinson

Founder, Director of Photography

An award-winning cameraman with 10 years' experience working for leading brands and broadcast networks. His work has appeared in ad campaigns in Times Square, for brands including Toyota, Lamborghini, Facebook and Uber. In broadcast news, he has worked with organisations including the BBC, CBS News and ABC.

Joie Chen

Head of Storytelling

An Emmy award-winning journalist who has spent her career telling compelling and captivating stories across America and the world. Best known for her decade as a primary anchor on CNN and CNN International, she has also served as a White House correspondent for CBS News and on the legendary “CBS Sunday Morning”, and as anchor and senior correspondent for Al Jazeera America's prime time program “America Tonight”.
